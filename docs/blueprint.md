# **App Name**: Amna Ali Portfolio

## Core Features:

- Project Showcase: Display Amna Ali's graphic design and digital marketing projects in an organized gallery.
- Skills Overview: A dedicated section to list and visually represent Amna Ali's skills in digital marketing and graphic design. Consider progress bars, or other informative representation.
- About Section: Detailed bio with a professional photo, highlighting Amna's experience, education, and approach to design and marketing.
- Contact Form: An easy-to-use form for potential clients to get in touch with Amna Ali, linked to her professional email address.
- Testimonials: Showcase testimonials from previous clients to build trust and credibility.
- AI Portfolio Description Generator: Use a generative AI tool to suggest descriptions of each project in the portfolio.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft teal (#99D8C5) to reflect creativity and professionalism.
- Background color: Light beige (#F5F5DC), a desaturated version of the primary color, for a clean, modern backdrop.
- Accent color: Warm coral (#FF7F50), an analogous hue to provide contrast for CTAs and important highlights.
- Font: 'Belleza' (sans-serif) for headings to convey style, paired with 'Alegreya' (serif) for body text to ensure readability.
- Use minimalist, professional icons to represent skills and categories within the portfolio.
- Employ a grid-based layout for project showcase to ensure a clean and structured presentation.
- Incorporate subtle transitions and hover effects on portfolio items to engage users.
